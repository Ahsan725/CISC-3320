Homework 3: Simulator
Report

Design policies: FCFS and SJF are non-preemptive. RR uses q=4. Priority is non-preemptive,
lower numeric priority is higher priority, ties by arrival then input order.
MLFQ uses Q0/Q1/Q2 with quanta 4/8/16, full-slice demotion and periodic boosts at t=50.
Priority aging can be enabled with --aging 5, but is disabled for the exact lecture benchmark.
